Lane Lines Privacy Policy

Updated September 27, 2026

Your information

Lane Lines saves the display name, username and profile photo you choose, results, event goals, meet and practice schedules, team names, locations you type, notes, checklists, custom events, and preferences, including your app mode, on your device. These details let you track your progress and plan training. Profiles and accounts in this version are local. Lane Lines does not upload your training records or profile to a developer-operated server, sell them, or use advertising.

Offline and Online modes

You choose a mode when you first open this version and can switch in Settings > App mode. Offline offers the original personal journal and does not require creating an account. Online opens the social preview and uses a username and password saved on this phone. Both modes use the same training records, schedule, and goals; switching does not delete or automatically publish them. Online is a local preview, not a live social network, cloud account, or synchronization service. Choosing Offline changes the app experience; it does not turn off your device's internet connection or the update and SDK services described below. The optional app guide is available in both modes and does not create training records or publish content.

Social preview information

Social preview athletes, friend activity, and sample posts are fictional. Your own preview posts, captions, selected photo references, comments, likes, saved posts, and sample connections are held in app memory for the current session. They are not transmitted to other people or saved in a developer-operated social database. Switching modes hides or shows the preview without publishing it. Restarting the app, signing out, or resetting the app clears this preview activity. Training-data backups do not include social preview activity. The system image picker may keep temporary copies of photos in app cache until the operating system clears them or you reset the app; Lane Lines does not upload these photos.

Local username and password

Online mode requires a local username and password; an Offline user can also choose to set up a local login in Settings. The app stores a salted password verifier and sign-in state in the operating system’s secure storage; it does not save your password as plain text or send it to a server. Once a local account is set up, it protects access to the same journal in either mode. Signing out does not delete your training records. This local login does not encrypt the training database or exported backups, and it does not provide online recovery or access on another phone. Credentials are excluded from training-data exports. Reset app removes the local account.

Photos and screenshot reading

You choose individual profile photos, post photos, and screenshots through the system photo picker. Lane Lines does not request access to your whole Android photo library, camera, or microphone. A copy of your profile photo is saved in app storage. Screenshots are temporarily copied to app cache so you can review them. Screenshot images and recognized text are processed on your device. On Android, Google ML Kit also sends technical diagnostics and usage information to Google, including device and app information, per-installation identifiers, feature events, errors, processing times, and input/output size and configuration. It does not send your screenshot images or recognized text to Google. On iOS, screenshot recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework.

App updates and third-party services

Installed builds may connect to Expo's EAS Update service over HTTPS to check for and download app updates. These requests include technical update information and expose your IP address to the service. Expo may collect operating-system information and randomized update tokens. No training records, profile photos, or screenshot contents are included in these update requests. Google and Expo process their service data under their own privacy policies. Lane Lines does not add its own advertising or behavioral analytics service.

Reminders and permissions

Notifications are optional and are scheduled locally by your device. You can disable them in Settings or your device's settings. Reminder titles and details may be visible on your lock screen depending on your device settings. On Android, optional Alarms & reminders access allows more precise practice reminders; the app remains usable without it. Lane Lines does not read your device location, contacts, or calendar. Locations entered by you can be opened in an external maps app.

Exports, sharing, and external links

Training data leaves the app when you choose to export a backup, share a personal-best card, or open an external link. You choose the destination using your device's share sheet or browser. The receiving app or website handles that information under its own privacy policy. Exported JSON backups are readable files and are not password-protected. Store them in a location you trust. Lane Lines does not automatically synchronize your records to another service.

Storage and security

Training records and preferences use app-local storage; profile images use app-local files. Lane Lines relies on your device's app isolation and security and does not add separate database or export-file encryption. Android automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup of app data are disabled. You can transfer records yourself by exporting and importing a backup. Service connections for updates and SDK diagnostics use HTTPS. Protect your device with a screen lock and current security updates.

Retention and deletion

Saved training data, profile details, and preferences stay until you delete them, reset the app, or uninstall it. Social preview activity is temporary as described above. Settings > Delete training data removes results, schedules, custom events, event goals, and their scheduled reminders while retaining your profile, local account, and preferences. Settings > Reset app additionally removes the local account, profile details, current and old app-owned profile photos, mode choice and other preferences, social preview activity, and temporary import/export/share files. Android uninstallation removes the app's local storage. Reset and uninstallation do not remove files you previously exported, information shared with other apps, or diagnostic/update information held by service providers; those providers govern their own retention and deletion.

Policy changes

This policy describes the current Offline journal and local Online social preview. A future live social network, cloud account, record synchronization, advertising, or different data handling would require updated disclosures before those features are introduced. Selecting Online in this version does not authorize future uploads of your existing records. The date above identifies the latest policy revision.
