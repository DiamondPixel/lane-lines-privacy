Lane Lines Privacy Policy

Updated September 26, 2026

Your information

Lane Lines saves the name or username and profile photo you choose, results, event goals, meet and practice schedules, team names, locations you type, notes, checklists, custom events, and preferences in this app on your device. These details let you track your progress and plan training. A profile is local; it does not create an online account. Lane Lines does not upload your training records or profile to a developer-operated server, sell them, or use advertising.

Photos and screenshot reading

You choose individual images through the system photo picker. Lane Lines does not request access to your whole Android photo library, camera, or microphone. A copy of your profile photo is saved in app storage. Screenshots are temporarily copied to app cache so you can review them. Screenshot images and recognized text are processed on your device. On Android, Google ML Kit also sends technical diagnostics and usage information to Google, including device and app information, per-installation identifiers, feature events, errors, processing times, and input/output size and configuration. It does not send your screenshot images or recognized text to Google. On iOS, screenshot recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework.

App updates and third-party services

Installed builds may connect to Expo's EAS Update service over HTTPS to check for and download app updates. These requests include technical update information and expose your IP address to the service. Expo may collect operating-system information and randomized update tokens. No training records, profile photos, or screenshot contents are included in these update requests. Google and Expo process their service data under their own privacy policies. Lane Lines does not add its own advertising or behavioral analytics service.

Reminders and permissions

Notifications are optional and are scheduled locally by your device. You can disable them in Settings or your device's settings. Reminder titles and details may be visible on your lock screen depending on your device settings. On Android, optional Alarms & reminders access allows more precise practice reminders; the app remains usable without it. Lane Lines does not read your device location, contacts, or calendar. Locations entered by you can be opened in an external maps app.

Exports, sharing, and external links

Training data leaves the app when you choose to export a backup, share a personal-best card, or open an external link. You choose the destination using your device's share sheet or browser. The receiving app or website handles that information under its own privacy policy. Exported JSON backups are readable files and are not password-protected. Store them in a location you trust. Lane Lines does not automatically synchronize your records to another service.

Storage and security

Training records and preferences use app-local storage; profile images use app-local files. Lane Lines relies on your device's app isolation and security and does not add separate database or export-file encryption. Android automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup of app data are disabled. You can transfer records yourself by exporting and importing a backup. Service connections for updates and SDK diagnostics use HTTPS. Protect your device with a screen lock and current security updates.

Retention and deletion

Your local information stays until you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall it. Settings > Delete training data removes results, schedules, custom events, event goals, and their scheduled reminders while retaining your profile and preferences. Settings > Reset app additionally removes profile details, current and old app-owned profile photos, preferences, and temporary import/export/share files. Android uninstallation removes the app's local storage. Reset and uninstallation do not remove files you previously exported, information shared with other apps, or diagnostic/update information held by service providers; those providers govern their own retention and deletion.

Policy changes

This policy describes the current local-storage version of Lane Lines. Future changes to accounts, sync, advertising, or other data handling will require updated disclosures. The date above identifies the latest policy revision.
