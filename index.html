Lane Lines Privacy Policy

Updated September 30, 2026

Your information

Lane Lines saves the display name and profile photo you choose, results, event goals, meet and practice schedules, team names, locations you type, notes, checklists, custom events, and preferences on your device. These details let you track your progress and plan training. This release does not require or create an account. Lane Lines does not upload your training records or profile to a developer-operated server, sell them, or use advertising. Optional Google Calendar sync is described below.

Offline journal

Lane Lines is a personal journal that works offline. It does not include a social feed, account creation, or result synchronization. You can optionally connect a Google calendar for your meet and practice schedule. The optional app guide does not create training records or publish content. Android screenshot recognition may send the technical diagnostics described below when your device has internet access. App updates are delivered through your app store.

Photos and screenshot reading

You choose individual profile photos and screenshots through the system photo picker. Lane Lines does not request access to your whole Android photo library, camera, or microphone. A copy of your profile photo is saved in app storage. Screenshots are temporarily copied to app cache so you can review them. Screenshot images and recognized text are processed on your device. On Android, Google ML Kit also sends technical diagnostics and usage information to Google, including device and app information, per-installation identifiers, feature events, errors, processing times, and input/output size and configuration. It does not send your screenshot images or recognized text to Google. On iOS, screenshot recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework.

App updates and third-party services

This release does not use over-the-air code updates from Expo. Android app updates are distributed through Google Play, which handles update requests under its own privacy policy. The Android screenshot recognition SDK may send technical diagnostics to Google as described above. If you choose Google Calendar sync, the device calendar service may sync your selected schedule entries with Google under Google's privacy policy. Lane Lines does not add its own advertising or behavioral analytics service.

Reminders and permissions

Notifications are optional and are scheduled locally by your device. You can disable them in Settings or your device's settings. Reminder titles and details may be visible on your lock screen depending on your device settings. On Android, optional Alarms & reminders access allows more precise practice reminders; the app remains usable without it. Lane Lines does not read your device location or contacts. Calendar access is requested only if you choose Google Calendar sync. Locations entered by you can be opened in an external maps app.

Optional Google Calendar sync

In Settings, you may grant calendar access and choose a writable Google calendar already connected to your phone. Lane Lines lists available calendar names and account labels, then creates, updates, or removes linked events for the meets and practices you save here. It sends event titles, times, locations, and a Lane Lines label; private notes, checklists, results, goals, and profile details are not placed in calendar events. The selected calendar and event links are saved on this device. Sync runs while the app is open or reopened; your device's Google Calendar service handles delivery to your Google account, which may require internet access and enabled calendar sync. Changes you make in Google Calendar are not imported into Lane Lines, and a later Lane Lines edit may overwrite the corresponding calendar entry. Disconnecting stops future updates but leaves existing Google Calendar entries in place. If you later choose another calendar, entries in the previous calendar also remain. You can revoke calendar permission in device settings.

Exports, sharing, and external links

Training data leaves the app when you choose to export a backup, share a personal-best card, open an external link, or connect Google Calendar for schedule sync. You choose the destination using your device's share sheet, browser, or calendar selection. The receiving app or website handles that information under its own privacy policy. Exported JSON backups are readable files and are not password-protected. Store them in a location you trust. Lane Lines does not synchronize results or profile data to another service.

Storage and security

Training records and preferences use app-local storage; profile images use app-local files. Lane Lines relies on your device's app isolation and security and does not add separate database or export-file encryption. Android automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup of app data are disabled. You can transfer records yourself by exporting and importing a backup. Protect your device with a screen lock and current security updates.

Retention and deletion

Saved training data, profile details, and preferences stay until you delete them, reset the app, or uninstall it. Settings > Delete training data removes results, schedules, custom events, event goals, and their scheduled reminders while retaining your profile and preferences. When calendar sync remains connected and permitted, Lane Lines removes linked calendar entries after their schedules are deleted and sync runs. Settings > Reset app additionally removes profile details, app-owned profile photos, preferences, calendar connection details, and temporary import/export/share files. Disconnecting, losing calendar access, resetting, or uninstalling the app may leave previously synced entries in Google Calendar, which you can remove there. Reset and uninstallation do not remove files you previously exported, information shared with other apps, or diagnostic information held by service providers; those providers govern their own retention and deletion.

Policy changes

This policy describes the current journal and optional Google Calendar schedule sync. A future social network, account system, result synchronization, advertising, or different data handling would require updated disclosures before those features are introduced. The date above identifies the latest policy revision.
